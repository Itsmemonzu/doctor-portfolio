# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, single page, no build step. Deployable to free static hosting.

## Users

Primary: patients and prospective patients who found the doctor's name (a referral, a Google result, a social bio link) and want to confirm who they are and how to reach them. Secondary: peers, journalists, and conference organisers checking credentials and talks.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for one doctor: who they are, what they practise, where they consult, their credentials, and every social or contact channel in one place. Success means a visitor leaves knowing the doctor's specialty and credibility, and taps through to book, call, or follow.

## Positioning

The doctor as a person with a practice, not a hospital listing: calm, precise, credible, and human.

## Operating Context

Mostly mobile, arriving from an Instagram/LinkedIn bio link, a WhatsApp forward, or search. Short visits; the contact and social links must be reachable within seconds.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English only.
- Doctor's real identity not supplied yet: all names, credentials, clinics, and handles are placeholders and must be labeled as such for replacement.
- No booking backend; links out to phone, email, WhatsApp, maps, and social profiles.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual pull from the user: cinematic dark atmospheric photography into crisp light surfaces (Superpower, Telepathic Instruments) and single-weight type with hairline structure (Integrated Biosciences, DNCO).
- Must not look like generic AI-generated medical templates (stock stethoscope, teal gradients, icon-tile grids).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real photos, credentials, testimonials, or metrics. Never fabricate claims presented as real; placeholders are labeled.

## Product Principles

- Reach in one tap: contact and socials are never more than one scroll away.
- Credibility through specifics, not adjectives.
- Calm over clinical: precise but warm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, real link text, works one-handed on a phone.
